## What's new

- Linty now checks for updates reliably while it lives in the menu bar. Earlier versions only completed that check, and the install of a required update, while the Linty window was open or right after your Mac woke, so an update could wait until you opened the window. If you are on 0.0.10 or 0.0.11 and this update has not appeared yet, open the Linty window once; it installs within a minute.
- No change to dictation, the recording overlay or your settings.
