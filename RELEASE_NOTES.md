## What's new

- The recording pill now appears the moment you press your trigger, even after Linty has been running for days. The overlay no longer grows in memory with every dictation, and it quietly refreshes itself between dictations as an extra safeguard, so quitting and reopening Linty is no longer needed to keep it responsive.
- Releasing the fn key (or your chosen modifier) now stops the microphone immediately, regardless of how busy the app is. Hold to talk, quick taps and hands-free listening with a double press work as before.
