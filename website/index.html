A little setup.
Then it’s just a key.

Will it run on my Mac?+

Linty requires macOS 14 or later and an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer). Your language, recording length, and available memory affect processing time.

What do I need to set up?+

Choose your spoken language. Linty downloads the speech support it needs, or reuses an existing download. Grant Microphone and Accessibility access, then choose your shortcut. System Check helps you try your microphone and dictation.

Can I change languages later?+

Yes. In Settings → Dictation, search 100 languages by English or native name, or choose Auto-detect and save up to three languages you use. Linty reuses downloaded speech support and downloads more only when needed. Your current language stays active until the new one is ready. App menus stay in English; recognition accuracy varies by language.

Does dictation need an internet connection?+

Internet access is needed to download speech support, optional text cleanup, and app updates. Once speech support is ready, dictation works offline. Your audio and transcripts stay on your Mac.

Is it free?+

Linty is free and open source under the MIT license. No account or subscription is needed for dictation or optional text cleanup.

What if a transcription isn’t right?+

Review and edit it in History, add unfamiliar names to your dictionary, and check that your spoken language is selected in Settings → Dictation. Use System Check to try your microphone. Audio quality and background noise can affect results.
